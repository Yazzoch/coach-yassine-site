The Coach

I Left Home To Build A Life.
Then I Built This.

I'm Yassine. Tunisian. Living in Kuala Lumpur.

I didn't grow up around gyms or personal trainers. I grew up around people who worked hard and didn't talk much about it. That shaped how I coach.

I found fitness the way most people do, as an escape. Something was heavy in my life and the gym was the one place where the weight made sense. Where progress was visible. Where the work you put in came back to you, every time.

That reliability, that honesty of the iron, became the thing I wanted to give other people.

I still remember the first client who told me they couldn't do it. Not physically couldn't. Believed they couldn't. They'd tried programs before. Start two weeks, disappear one week, feel ashamed, restart. The cycle was the problem, not their body.

That client became one of my proudest results. Not because of the kilos lost. Because one morning they messaged me and said: "I never thought this version of me existed."

That's what I'm here for.

I work with a small number of clients on purpose, because you deserve a coach who actually knows your week. Your program changes every single week, based on your real data. Not a template, not a copy-paste. And when you message me, I reply. Not an assistant. Me.
